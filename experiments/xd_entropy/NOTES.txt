ENTROPY METHOD AND PERMANENT-TYPE BOUNDS FOR a(N)                    (experiments/xd_entropy/)
=========================================================================================
Labels: [Lean] = proven in Folds/XdEntropy.lean (no sorry, no native_decide; axioms propext,
Classical.choice, Quot.sound).  [paper] = proved here, not formalised.  [sketch] = argument
written down but neither formalised nor checked numerically.  [data N<=..] = computed.
[conjecture].  Notation: t_m = time of panel m, r = reach, W_m = window of m (2r+1 panels),
S_k = first k panels, f_k = N-k, V_k = number of valid moves at S_k, bad_k = f_k - V_k.

SUMMARY
-------
* Target "a(N) <= N! e^{-cN} with c > 0.1255": NOT reached for even N.  Reached for nothing
  beyond a constant; e^{-omega(N)} remains open.
* New rigorous bound, valid for EVERY N [Lean: semiCount_le_kick]:
        a(N) <= c! c^(N-c)   whenever (N-c)(N-c+1) <= E(N),
  E(N) = #{x<y : y-x <= min(reach x, reach y)} ~ N^2/6 (pairs lying in each other's windows).
  This is N! exp(-(0.11642+o(1))N), 0.11642 = -ln(c0)-1+c0, c0 = 1-1/sqrt(6).
  For ODD N it improves the known constant 0.0878 to 0.1164 (0.1168 at N=4097 when combined
  with the MaxMoves2 caps through semiCount_le_budget).  For EVEN N it is weaker than the
  known 0.1255 and combining does not help (section 4).
* The panel-by-panel entropy method proposed in the brief (reveal t_m after its window, count
  admissible ranks) caps out: at most floor(log2 N)+1 panels per half can be revealed after
  their own window [Lean], so it yields at most exp(-Theta(log^2 N)) (section 2).

1. ADMISSIBLE RANKS (the random-walk lemma)
-------------------------------------------
Reveal t_m after the other 2r panels of W_m.  Sort those by time and write L/R for the arm.
The rank j of t_m among them (j = 0..2r) is admissible iff the first j letters contain as
many L as R, i.e. iff the bridge L=+1, R=-1 of length 2r is at 0 at time j.
 [Lean] zero_mem_admissible, top_mem_admissible: ranks 0 and 2r are always admissible.
 [Lean] card_admissible_le: at most r+1 of the 2r+1 ranks (zeros sit at even times; attained
        by LRLR...).  So the worst case saves only a factor ~2 per panel.
 [Lean] card_wallis_middle: summed over all (2r)! arrangements the number of admissible ranks
        is 4^r (r!)^2, i.e. the mean is 4^r / C(2r,r) = sqrt(pi r)(1+o(1)) (not c sqrt(r) with
        another constant: it is exactly the Wallis factor times 2r+1).  This is the identity
        sum_j C(2j,j) C(2r-2j,r-j) = 4^r (Wallis.sum_centralBinom_antidiagonal).
 Remark [paper].  When the revealed set R contains W_m \ {m} and more, the admissible VALUES
 are the unrevealed values lying in a zero-gap of the bridge; when W_m \ {j} is revealed for
 some j != m (and t_m is revealed), C_m forces the single bit [t_j < t_m].  These two are the
 only inferences from a completed window.

2. THE CAP OF "WINDOW-LAST" ORDERS
----------------------------------
 [Lean] last_in_window_double: for ANY revelation order, if two left-half panels m < m' are
        both revealed after all other panels of their windows, then m' >= 2m.  (m lies in
        W_{m'} = [1,2m'-1]; if m' <= 2m-1 then m' lies in W_m.)  By mirror symmetry the same
        holds in the right half.  Hence at most floor(log2 N)+1 panels per half are
        "last in their own window".
 Consequence [paper].  The admissible-rank lemma can be applied to O(log N) panels only; the
 centre-out, ends-in and random orders make no difference.  For the dyadic chain m = 2^j the
 windows are pairwise compatible, the constraints are exactly independent
 (Folds/Independence.lean) and the bound is the dyadic Wallis product
 N! prod_j w(2^j) = N! exp(-Theta(log^2 N)), already known (semiCount_mul_le_dyadic).
 So this route cannot give even e^{-cN}.
 [Lean] card_windowsThrough_ge: every panel lies in >= floor(N/2) windows.  Hence the panel
        revealed last completes >= N/2 windows simultaneously, and at most ceil(N/2)+1 windows
        can be completed at pairwise different moments in any order.
 [paper] In an order where W_m is completed by j < m (outer arm), j also completes every
        window [1,2m'-1] with j <= 2m'-1 <= 2m-1: such completions come in clumps of size
        >= m/2, and by the same doubling argument only O(log N) panels can complete a window
        from the outer arm or the centre.  All useful completions are by an inner-arm panel
        close to 2m-1, i.e. the order is essentially left-to-right on the left half.
 [paper] Uniformly random order (Linial-Luria style) is bad here for the same reason: j
        completes some left window iff it is a record, probability ~1/j, and then it
        completes a whole clump.  Expected saving O(log^2 N) in total.
 [sketch] Left-to-right order.  At j = 2m-1 the constraint C_m forces [t_j < t_m]; the
        number of admissible relative ranks is 2A_m (forced below) or 2(r-A_m) (forced
        above), A_m = number of left-arm panels placed before m.  The pointwise bound
        a(N) <= max_t prod_j N_j(t) then saves a factor only when the forced side is not
        the large side.  A clique/pigeonhole argument on the blocks (M,2M] (same-type panels
        of a block see each other) gives a saving >= ~0.03 N: weaker than the known bounds.
        One forced bit per panel also means that no order of this kind can save more than
        O(N) pointwise.  Getting more would need the distribution of [t_j < t_m] under the
        uniform valid measure, which is the original problem.
 Why Bregman/Radhakrishnan does not transfer [paper]: there the number of choices at the
 reveal time is uniformly distributed over the random order for EVERY fixed matching.  Here
 the number of admissible ranks (zeros of the bridge of m) depends on the sequence itself,
 between 1.. and r+1, and no symmetrisation makes it sequence-independent; an adversarial
 (low-discrepancy) sequence has many zeros.  Linial-Luria works because every alternative is
 killed by a fixed number of revealed elements; here an alternative value v for t_j is
 killed only by panels m with t_m between t_j and v whose windows are complete, a
 sequence-dependent set.

3. THE KICK BUDGET (a time-averaged parity bound)                                   [Lean]
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Time order.  Path form of the tree bound (PathLemma.lean): a(N) <= max over valid sequences
of prod_k V_k.  Instead of bounding each V_k by a per-state maximum, bound the TOTAL
deficiency sum_k bad_k along the path and use AM-GM.
 (K1) kicked_not_both, kicked_card_add: when x = a_k is placed, every free panel m with
      x in W_m changes the parity of |S cap W_m|; a valid move needs an even count
      (window_count_even), so m is invalid before or after the step:
            bad_{k-1} + bad_k >= c_k := #{m free after step k : a_k in W_m}.
 (K2) card_mutualPairs_le_sum_kicked: sum_k c_k = #{(x,m): x in W_m, t_x < t_m} >= E(N), since
      a pair of panels lying in each other's windows is counted in one of the two orders.
 (K3) sum_moves_le:  E(N) + 2 sum_k V_k <= 2 sum_k (N-k), i.e. sum_k bad_k >= E(N)/2.
 (K4) pathProd_le_of_budget (weighted AM-GM, via x <= e^{x-1}): if V_k <= B_k <= N-k and
      2 sum (B_k - c)^+ + 2 sum (N-k-B_k) <= E(N) then prod V_k <= prod min(B_k, c).
 semiCount_le_budget: the resulting bound for a(N) with arbitrary per-state caps B.
 semiCount_le_kick:   B_k = N-k:  a(N) <= prod_{k<N} min(N-k, c) = c! c^(N-c)
                      whenever 2 sum_{k<N} (N-k-c)^+ = (N-c)(N-c+1) <= E(N).
 semiCount_ten_le:    instance N = 10 (E = 12, c = 7): a(10) <= 1728720.
 [data N<=10] kick_check.py: (K1), (K2) and the final bound hold for every valid sequence.
 [data] kick_check.py, budget_const.py: E(N)/N^2 = .1589, .1647, .1662, .1665 at N = 64, 256,
        1024, 4096 (limit 1/6); saving/N of the kick bound: .1086, .1134, .1153, .1163
        (limit 0.11642).

4. COMBINATION WITH THE KNOWN CAPS, AND WHY EVEN N DOES NOT IMPROVE     [data, budget_const.py]
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
With B = the MaxMoves2 caps (parity at odd levels, 2-adic bound with size term at even ones):
      N     caps only   kick only   caps + kick
     1024    0.1256      0.1153      0.1256
     1025    0.0878      0.1157      0.1161
     4096    0.1255      0.1163      0.1255
     4097    0.0878      0.1162      0.1168
 Even N: the caps already force sum bad_k = 0.512 E(N) > E(N)/2, so the budget is not
 binding: no gain.  Reason: (K1) is the path-wise form of the same parity fact that gives
 V <= N/2 at odd levels, and an adversary may pay the kick budget with exactly the panels
 that the 2-adic caps forbid.  Odd N: the caps force only 0.387 E(N), the budget binds.
 NEGATIVE: c > 0.1255 for even N was not obtained.

5. HOW MUCH ROOM IS LEFT IN THE BUDGET                               [data, minpath.c, exact DP]
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Minimum over all valid sequences (subset DP), compared with the proven bounds:
      N                       8     12     16     20     22     23     24
  E(N)                        7     18     35     57     70     77     84
  min sum c_k                10     22     44     70     86     94    102
  min sum odd_k               6     13     28     44     54     56     63
  min sum bad_k               6     17     34     57     71     78     85
  min sum c_k  / E          1.43   1.22   1.26   1.23   1.23   1.22   1.21
  min sum odd_k / (E/2)     1.71   1.44   1.60   1.54   1.54   1.45   1.50
  min sum bad_k / (E/2)     1.71   1.89   1.94   2.00   2.03   2.03   2.02
(odd_k = number of free panels with odd window count; bad_k >= odd_k >= ... >= E/2 is the
proven chain.  The three minima are attained by different sequences.)
 * (K2) has about 21% slack: validity (balance of the two arms) forces kicks between panels
   that do not see each other mutually.
 * (K1) has more: sum odd_k is ~1.5 E/2, and sum bad_k is ~2.0 E/2 on this range, i.e.
   min sum bad_k ~ E(N) ([data N<=24]; in units of N^2 this is .148 at N = 24 and rising
   with E(N)/N^2 -> 1/6).
 A budget D = delta N^2 gives saving (-ln c - 1 + c) N with c = 1 - sqrt(2 delta):
   delta = 1/12 (proven): 0.116;  0.0886: 0.1255 (break-even with the even-N caps);
   delta = 1/8 (ratio 1.5): 0.193;  delta = 1/6 (ratio 2.0): 0.284.
 [conjecture, data N<=24] sum_k bad_k >= E(N) (1-o(1)) for every valid sequence; it would give
   a(N) <= N! e^{-0.28 N} for all N.  Even "ratio >= 1.07" would beat 0.1255 for even N.
   (If the Path Lemma holds, sum bad_k / N^2 tends to 1/2.)
 Where the slack in (K1) is [paper].  Exactly, sum_k odd_k = (1/2) sum_k c_k + U, where U counts
 pairs (k, m) with m free, not kicked at step k and odd (an "odd gap" of m).  U = 0 would need
 the window panels of every m to be placed in consecutive pairs.  In block coordinates
 (blocks {2i,2i+1}, N even) the window parity of m is a prefix sum of block parities, and after
 an odd step in block b the valid next moves are the left panels <= b and the right panels
 > b + N/2: the block-mate 2b+1 is never among them.  So kicks cannot be paired by filling
 blocks.  Turning this into a proof of U >= eta N^2 was not achieved; it is the concrete open
 step that would give c > 0.1255 for every N.
 [sketch] The linear relaxation of (K2) with the balance constraints but without
 transitivity of the time order has value E(N), so an improvement of (K2) must use
 transitivity.

6. NOT DONE / NEGATIVE
----------------------
 * Exact conditional entropies H(t_m | revealed) by enumeration for several orders (item 1 of
   the brief) were not computed; section 2 shows which orders can matter (only essentially
   left-to-right ones), and section 5 gives the corresponding path-wise quantities instead.
 * No lower-bound (randomised greedy / max-entropy Markov) result; nothing to compare with
   the slip exponent 1/16.
 * Shearer / Bregman-type product: no valid product over all panels was found.  Shearer over
   coordinate sets loses about w(1 - w/2N) nats per window of size w against a saving of
   (1/2) ln(pi r), so it is useless for small windows; the only valid Wallis-type products are
   over compatible chains (O(log N) factors).  The kick bound prod_k min(N-k, c) is the
   product-form bound obtained instead (a product over time steps, not panels).
 * e^{-omega(N)}: open.  Every bound here saves O(N).
