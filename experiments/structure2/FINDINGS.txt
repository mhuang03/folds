STRUCTURE OF THE REFINED SEQUENCES                                   (experiments/structure2/)
==========================================================================================
Question: are the refined objects (first-panel counts, first-pair counts, prefix counts,
reachable sets) nicer than a(N)?  What are the exact shapes of r(N) = a(N)/a(N-1) and of the
correction y(N) = ln a(N) - ln a_W(N)?  How do the prime factors of a(N) behave?  Are there
exact cross-sequence relations?  All runs used 1-2 cores and < 3 GB of RAM.

Notation.  F(N,m) = #valid sequences starting with panel m;  L(N,m) = #ending with m;
F2(N,m,x) = #starting with (m,x);  T(N,k) = #valid k-prefixes;  R(N,k) = #reachable k-sets;
A(N,k) = #alive k-sets (reachable and completable);  c(N) = self-dual count;  J, I as in
../jseq/NOTES.txt;  s(N) = the symmetric count (A003407).  "[proven]" = proved here or in
Lean (Folds/Structure2.lean), "[data]" = checked on the stated range, "[conj]" = conjecture.

SUMMARY
 1. L(N,m) = F(N,m) (reversal), F(N,m) = F(N,N+1-m) (mirror), and for even N exactly half of
    the valid sequences start with an odd panel.  [proven, Lean]  The (1,x) starts are exactly
    those with 2x >= N+2.  [proven, Lean]  Every other exact identity tested failed.
 2. THE FIRST-PANEL LAW HAS A LIMIT SHAPE.  Var(first panel)/N^2 = 0.0574 +- 0.0001 for every
    N = 18..30 (no drift).  The law is explained to total variation ~0.025 by the Wallis
    (independence) heuristic P(last = m) ∝ 1/w(r_m), whose continuum limit is the density
    h(x) = (3/sqrt 2) sqrt(min(x,1-x)) with variance 1/17.5 = 0.05714.  [data / conj]
    Only the first three panels at each edge deviate strongly (ratio to Wallis 0.60, 0.91,
    0.86 at m = 1, 2, 3; N = 30).  N*F(N,1)/a(N) -> 0 slowly (0.37 at N = 12, 0.20 at N = 30).
 3. SYMMETRIC CASE (A003407): exactly half of the 3-AP-free sequences start with an odd panel,
    for EVERY N <= 30, odd N included.  [conj, data N <= 30]  For odd N the k-th panel is
    parity-balanced for every step k <= (N+1)/4 (N <= 11, brute force).  In the semi case
    this balance fails for every odd N >= 7.  sym F(N,1) and F(N,ceil(N/2)) are OEIS
    A296530, A296531 (consistency check of the sym data).
 4. y(N) = ln a - ln a_W  =  smooth  -  0.081*[N odd]  +  0.67*[3|N]/N  +  noise(sd 0.008)
    on N = 10..38.  The parity offset is a CONSTANT (not decaying); the 3|N bonus is better fit
    by 0.67/N than by a constant (AIC -273 vs -266).
 5. r(N)/N is not alpha + beta/ln N with alpha > 0: on the SMC estimates to N = 4096,
    (r/N)*ln N falls slowly, 0.96 (N ~ 35) -> 0.83 (N ~ 2900), i.e. r(N) ~ N/(kappa ln N) with
    kappa slowly increasing, effective r/N ~ (ln N)^{-1.18} over 35..4096.
 6. Prime factors of a(N): typical of random integers; no prime p <= 50 divides a(N) on a
    residue class mod q <= 12 beyond 2 and 4 | a(N) (N = 3 mod 4) (already proven).
 7. No exact linear relation with polynomial coefficients among a, c, J, I, F(N,1), F(N,2),
    F(N,mid), T(N,N-2), T(N,N-3), R, F2(N,1,N), the semi parity defect E-O, s, sym F(N,1):
    1838 overdetermined searches (>= 4 spare equations each), none found.
 8. R(N,k) (reachable sets by size) is log-concave in k for every N <= 30.  [conj]  (Not
    real-rooted; the symmetric analogue is not log-concave from N = 8.)

1.2 Exact facts.  [proven; Lean: Folds/Structure2.lean]
 * L(N,m) = F(N,m): reversal is a bijection on valid sequences.  So the "ending with m"
   triangle is the same object as the "starting with m" one.
 * F(N,m) = F(N,N+1-m) (mirror).
 * For even N: #{first panel odd} = #{first panel even} = a(N)/2 (the mirror swaps parity
   when N+1 is odd).  For odd N the mirror preserves parity and the balance FAILS in the semi
   case: E - O = #even-first - #odd-first =
     20, 148, 2312, 56344, 1680608, 41779316, 1654712136, 53436724154, 2559348278480,
     146989716562612, 7818662696900268, 522220844799116936   (N = 7, 9, ..., 29),
   (E-O)/a = 0.078, 0.035, 0.033, 0.035, 0.035, 0.028, 0.030, 0.021, 0.022, 0.022, 0.019, 0.019,
   slowly decreasing.  For N = 3, 5 it is 0.  Odd-first sequences are rarer.  Not in OEIS.
 * F2(N,1,x) > 0 only if 2x >= N+2 (proven: panel 1 lies in the left arm of x iff 2x <= N+1),
   and F2(N,1,N) = 0 for even N (the dead prefix {1,N}, ../live).  For odd N all of
   x = (N+3)/2..N occur (data N <= 30).  The F2-zero pattern: the zero ordered pairs are
   exactly the invalid 2-prefixes plus (1,N), (N,1) for even N (N <= 30).

1.3 The first-panel law (firstlaw.py).  Write p_N(m) = F(N,m)/a(N).
   N     8      12     16     20     24     26     28     30
  Var/N^2 .0595 .0590 .0581 .0575 .0575 .0574 .0573 .0574
  It is flat at 0.0574 from N = 18 on.  The heuristic: the LAST panel's constraint is free
  (full window), so under independence P(last = m) ∝ 1/w(r_m), w(r) = 4^r r!^2/(2r+1)! (the
  proven single-panel Wallis factor).  This model has Var_W/N^2 = 0.0628 (N=8) .. 0.0589
  (N=30) -> 1/17.5 = 0.057143.  Exact law vs model at N = 30 (N p(m), N q(m), ratio):
     m   1     2     3     4     5     6     8     10    12    14    15
     p .201  .456  .542  .796  .887  .952  1.107 1.209 1.305 1.359 1.424
     q .335  .502  .628  .733  .824  .907  1.052 1.180 1.296 1.402 1.452
     r .60   .91   .86   1.09  1.08  1.05  1.05  1.02  1.01  .97   .98
  TV distance 0.023-0.033 for N = 12..30, not shrinking fast.  So the exact law is the
  Wallis law with a boundary layer at m = 1, 2, 3 (and their mirrors): placing panel 1, 2 or 3
  first puts a point into the left arm of almost every left-half window and is penalised;
  panels 4..9 pick up the slack.
  CONJECTURE (first-panel limit law).  N*p_N(xN) -> h(x) = (3/sqrt 2) sqrt(min(x,1-x)) in the
  bulk, so Var/N^2 -> 1/17.5.  The data are consistent with this (0.0574 vs 0.05714), but the
  approach is not visible within N <= 30, so the limit might also be ~0.0574.  The middle
  value N*p_N(ceil(N/2)) = 1.38-1.45 (N = 24..30), against h(1/2) = 1.5.
  Edge columns: F(N,m)/F(N,1) for fixed m drift slowly upwards (m = 4: 3.4 -> 3.95, m = 6:
  3.7 -> 4.7, N = 12..30); N*F(N,1)/a(N) = 0.374 (12), 0.273 (20), 0.201 (30): neither
  constant*ln N nor sqrt N scaling holds.  The decay is slow, about N^-0.7 locally.
  F(N,1)/F(N,2) = 0.44-0.47 for N = 11..30 (no visible trend).

1.4 Symmetric analogue (data/tri_sym.txt; sym F rows in data/symstats_N.txt).
 * Exactly half of all 3-AP-free sequences start with an odd panel, for every N <= 28
   (odd N included; sym stats for N = 29, 30 also checked).  [conj]
   Brute force (parity_pos.py) for the k-th panel: at odd N the odd-even difference is 0 for
   k <= (N+1)/4 and k >= N+1-(N+1)/4 and nonzero in the middle, e.g. N = 11:
   [0,0,0,44,416,1540,416,44,0,0,0].  For the semi rule it is nonzero already at k = 1
   (N = 7: [-20,24,32,184,32,24,-20]).  No proof found: the obvious involution (swap the first
   two panels) is not always available.  If true, this is a structural property of 3-AP-free
   permutations that the semi-symmetric relaxation does NOT share.
 * Many accidental-looking equalities F(N,m) = F(N,m') with m' != N+1-m, e.g. pairs (1,2),
   (3,4), ... at N = 14, 18, 22, 26 (N = 2 mod 4), palindromic 4-blocks at N = 20, 28,
   triples (1,3) at N = 19, 27.  None occur in the semi case.  Not analysed further.
 * The sym first-panel law has a plateau in the middle: N*p(m) ~ 2.2-2.4 for
   max(m, N+1-m) <= 16 versus 0.5-1.0 outside (N = 25..28).  The jump sits at a power of 2,
   which reflects the binary structure of 3-AP-free orderings.  The semi law is smooth.
 * sym F(N,1) = A296530 (non-averaging permutations with first element n) and sym
   F(N,ceil(N/2)) = A296531; the other sym columns are not in OEIS.

1.5 Prefix counts and sets.
 * T(N,N-2)/a(N) -> ~0.969 (0.9684 at N = 29); T(N,N-3)/a(N) -> ~0.573 (0.5725 at N = 29).
   Mean branching T(N,k)/T(N,k-1) alternates with the parity of k in the second half
   (N = 28: ... 3.34, 3.36, 2.75, 2.81, 2.23, 2.25, 1.59, 1.69, 1.03, 1.00), the parity
   lemma at work.
 * Reachable sets: sum_k R(N,k)/2^N = 0.8188 (N = 26), 0.8182 (N = 29), slowly decreasing; alive

   quasi-polynomial, cf. ../prefix V(N,3)).
 * Row polynomials (rowpolys.py): F_N(t), T_N(t), R_N(t) are never real-rooted (N <= 30).
   R_N(t) is log-concave for every N <= 30 [conj: always]; T_N(t) is not log-concave from
   N = 11; F_N(t) is not even unimodal for most odd N (N = 9, 13, 15, 21, 23, 25, 27, 29),
   because of small dips like F(9,3) < F(9,2).  The alternating sums R_N(-1) =
   -1, -2, -2, -6, -6, -15, -13, -39, -43, -99, -129, -363, -428, -1033, -1248, -3119, ...
   (N = 4..19) are not in OEIS.
 * No recurrence in N along fixed m (m = 1, 2, 3), along m = ceil(N/2) or N/2, or along the

 CONJECTURE.  ln a(N) = ln a_W(N) + S(N) - delta [N odd] + gamma [3|N]/N + o(1/N), with S
 smooth, delta = 0.081 +- 0.004 and gamma = 0.67 +- 0.07.  Equivalently
     r(N)/N = rho(N) * exp(-+2 delta) * (1 + O([3|N]/N)),  with + for even N and - for odd N.
 The parity offset does not decay (it is the same on N = 10..24 and N = 24..38), so it is a
 genuine O(1) multiplicative constant e^{-0.081} = 0.922 for odd N relative to the even
 interpolation.  The Wallis model itself already contains the middle-panel factor, so this
 is an extra correlation effect of the middle panel (it is placed only at odd steps).
 The bump b(N) = y(N) - (y(N-2)+y(N+2))/2: N*b(N) is +0.19..+0.55 for 3 | N and -0.4..-1.0
 otherwise (N = 12..36), consistent with an O(1/N) arithmetic term on top of the curvature.
 Within residue classes N*[y(N) - (y(N-1)+y(N+1))/2] (N >= 20) grows slightly
 (e.g. N = 0 mod 6: 2.47, 3.12, 3.87), so the alternation in y is not exactly
 constant + c/N.  The data cannot separate this from the smooth part.

2.2 Far out (SMC, ../asymptotics NOTES 3.2).  Interval geometric means of r(N)/N:
     interval   32-38  48-64  96-128 192-256 384-512 768-1024 1536-2048 2048-4096
     r/N        .271   .238   .201   .173    .152    .130     .116      .104
     (r/N) ln N .962   .954   .944   .932    .929    .883     .869      .830
 So r(N)/N -> 0.  An expansion alpha + beta/ln N + ... fitted on these points gives
 alpha = -0.03, i.e. alpha = 0 within the errors.  The natural form is r(N) = N/(kappa(N) ln N)
 with kappa(N) = 1.04 (N ~ 35) .. 1.20 (N ~ 2900), slowly increasing.  This is the
 discrete-derivative version of the README conjecture a(N) = N!/(kappa ln N)^N.  The SMC
 form D of ../asymptotics (d(N) = 1.69 lnln N - 1.675 + 1.83/ln N) predicts r/N = 0.263 at
 N = 38, against the exact mean of the parity classes 0.265.  The periodic parts cannot be
 seen at SMC precision.

2.3 a(N)/(N a(N-1)) = r(N)/N (same sequence) and y(N)/N = 0.132 at N = 38, still rising
 (README: log(a/a_W) ~ 0.09N earlier); y/N increases by ~0.004 per step at N ~ 35.

4. CROSS-SEQUENCE RELATIONS
---------------------------
(relations.py, data/relations_out.txt)
 Search: for each target X in {a, c, F(N,1), F(N,2), F(N,mid), T(N,N-2), T(N,N-3), E-O,
 J, F2(N,1,N), R, s, sym F(N,1)} and pools of 0-2 of {a, c, F(N,1), F(N,mid), T(N,N-2), J},
 the exact rational nullspace of [N^p Y(N-j)], j <= J, p <= P, with (J,P) in
 {(1,0),(2,0),(1,1),(3,0),(2,1),(1,2),(4,0),(3,1)}, over all N where the terms exist
 (N >= 6), keeping only systems with >= 4 more equations than unknowns.  1838 systems:
 NO relation.  Adding convolution terms (sum a(i)a(N-i), a(floor N/2)a(ceil N/2), 2^N, N!) to
 a, c, J, F(N,1), T(N,N-2): no relation.
 c(2n) versus a(n): c(2n)/a(n)^2 -> 0 quickly (1.4e-5 at n = 15).  The right scale is
 c(2n) ~ sqrt(a(2n)): c(2n)^2/a(2n) = 3.6, 1.5, 5.2, 4.2, 3.1, 3.5, 5.6, 3.1, 6.0, 5.0, 4.4,
 5.0, 6.4, 4.1, 6.2, 5.6, 5.2, 5.7 (n = 2..19), bounded but irregular (cf. ../arith).
 Conclusion: the refined counts are as "transcendental" as a(N) itself.  The only exact
 identities are the symmetry ones of 1.2 and the known (A), (B) decompositions.


5. SYMMETRIC ANALOGUE (summary of what transfers)
-------------------------------------------------
 transfers: reversal/mirror identities; F2 support rules for (1,x) (in the sym case (1,x) is
   valid iff 2x >= N+2 as well); the first-panel law has a bulk-plus-boundary shape.
 does not transfer: the parity balance of the first panel at odd N holds for sym (N <= 30) but
   not for semi; R log-concavity holds for semi (N <= 30) but not for sym; sym F rows show
   power-of-2 plateaus and many exact coincidences, semi rows are smooth.
 Neither has any column/diagonal recurrence or OEIS match except the two known sym columns.


6. NEGATIVE RESULTS (collected)
 * No recurrence (constant or polynomial coefficients, up to 4 shifts) for any column,
   diagonal or row sum; no relation to a of smaller size.
 * No real-rooted row polynomials.
 * No OEIS match for any semi refined sequence (23 tried) or derived one (E-O, R_N(-1)).
 * No residue-class divisibility beyond the proven 2-adic facts; prime factors look random.
 * The mod-6 effect is not a pure c_r/N periodic correction; a constant parity term plus a
   [3|N]/N term is the best small model.


7. LEAN  (Folds/Structure2.lean, no sorry, no native_decide; axioms: propext,
   Classical.choice, Quot.sound only; build with `bash leanbuild.sh Folds.Structure2`)

 These are the only exact identities found; the interesting statements (the first-panel
 limit law, the sym parity balance at odd N, R log-concavity) are conjectures.
