Conjecture E_k: dead sets that avoid the end panels
===================================================

Notation as in experiments/livek.  Panels [N] = {1..N}; reach(m) = min(m-1, N-m); arms
L(m) = [m - reach, m - 1], R(m) = [m + 1, m + reach].  A set D is CO-REACHABLE (Lean
`Live2.CoReach`) if [N] \ D has a semi-symmetric ordering from scratch; equivalently
(`Ek.bg_of_coReach`, `Adjacent.coReach_of_inward`'s proof) the free panels can be placed one at a
time on the BACKGROUND D, every step valid (|placed n L(m)| = |placed n R(m)|).  DEAD = not
co-reachable.  A valid prefix is live iff its set is co-reachable, so dead sets and dead prefixes
are the same question.  "Lean" means Folds/Ek.lean (imports Folds.LiveK and Folds.Live7; no sorry,
no native_decide, builds in about 6 s).

STATUS (short)
  * Conjecture E_k as stated (finitely many exceptions in [2, N-1]) is FALSE for every k >= 5:
    the B4 family {2, 2j, 2j+1, 2j+2, 2j+3} (j >= 3, 2j+3 <= N-1) is dead for every N (Lean),
    so there are at least floor((N-8)/2) dead 5-subsets of [2, N-1] up to mirror image
    (`not_finite_exceptions_five`).  More generally, for every a >= 1, the anchored blocks
    {a} u [2j, 2j+2a-1] (j >= a+1) are dead (Lean, `not_coReach_anchor`), so dead (2a+1)-sets
    can have ALL elements at distance >= a from the ends (`exists_dead_far`).
  * E_k holds numerically for k <= 4 (k = 2, 3: N <= 100; k = 4: N <= 40) with the single
    exception {2, 6, 7, 11} at N = 12, as conjectured in livek.
  * k = 5, N <= 40: the dead 5-subsets of [2, N-1] are EXACTLY the B4 sets, their mirror images,
    and the symmetric sporadic set {2, 6, 7, 8, 12} at N = 13 (all proven dead: B4 in Lean,
    the sporadic one by exhaustive search).
  * The exceptions that are not explained by a family near an end are all mirror-symmetric and
    form one more family S_a (section 5): {a, N+1-a} u [2a+2, N-2a-1] at N = 6a and N = 6a+1
    ({2, 6, 7, 11} = S_2 at N = 12 is the exception of E_4).  Dead for a <= 6 (exhaustive).
  * REVISED CONJECTURE E*_k (section 6): with c_k = max(1, floor((k-1)/2)), every k-subset of
    [c_k + 1, N - c_k] is co-reachable, except S_{k/2} at N = 3k for even k >= 4.  Verified:
    k = 2, 3 (N <= 100), 4 (N <= 40), 5 (N <= 40), 6 (N <= 32), 7 (N <= 28), 8 (N <= 24).
    The radius c_k is optimal up to the exceptions: the anchored blocks B_a give dead
    (2a+1)-sets with least element a = c_{2a+1}.
  * Counting corollary (Lean, `card_dead_le`): if every dead k-subset of [N] meets
    [1, c] u [N+1-c, N] or lies in E, the number of dead k-subsets is at most
    2c C(N-1, k-1) + |E|.  So E*_k gives  #dead k-sets <= 2 c_k C(N-1, k-1) + 1 = O(N^{k-1}), and
    hence live / valid -> 1 for every fixed k.  `card_dead_le_of_Ek` is the c = 1 form asked for
    (2 C(N-1, k-1) + |E|), conditional on E_k (true numerically for k <= 4, false for k >= 5);
    `card_dead_pairs_le_64`: unconditionally at most 2(N-1) dead pairs for N <= 64 (Conjecture C,
    Live7).
  * Proof of E_k itself: not obtained.  The structural part that IS proven explains why the ends
    matter (sections 2-4), and section 7 discusses the three suggested strategies.


1. Numerical results (ek.c; 1 core; outputs k*.txt)
---------------------------------------------------
ek N k lo: every k-subset of [lo, N+1-lo], up to mirror image, is decided by
  (1) Theorem G for some centre;  (2) DFS witnesses on the background D, in the order of the
  distance to 8 fixed centres, with Theorem G tested at every node (if the placed set satisfies
  G, the centre greedy completes it);  (3) the LOCAL RELAXATION dead test (below), at both ends,
  radius w <= 13;  (4) 60 randomized DFS restarts;  (5) exhaustive DFS with memo (certifies dead).

  windows lie in [1, 2w-1]; the free panels of [w+1, 2w-1] are wildcards that may be placed at
  any moment; panels beyond 2w-1 are ignored.  If no relaxed play places the whole core, D is

2. The background form and the end panels (Lean)
------------------------------------------------
`bg_of_coReach`: CoReach N D gives a list a of the free panels with every step valid on D plus
the earlier steps (the ordering from scratch read backwards; `semiStep_compl`).
`coReach_of_insert`: if x is free and valid on the background D, then CoReach N (D u {x}) implies
CoReach N D (append x to the from-scratch ordering of the complement of D u {x}; its step is
valid there iff valid on D, by complementation).  End panels have empty windows, so
`coReach_of_insert_one`, `coReach_of_insert_N`, and contrapositively `not_coReach_insert_one`,
`not_coReach_insert_N`: a dead set avoiding 1 stays dead when 1 is added.  This is why the dead
families "containing 1 or N plus anything" exist: every dead set avoiding the ends produces dead
sets containing them, never the converse.


3. The anchored-block family B_a (Lean, all a, j, N)
----------------------------------------------------
THEOREM (`not_coReach_anchor`).  Let a >= 1, j >= a + 1, 2j + 2a - 1 <= N, and let D contain a and
the block [2j, 2j + 2a - 1], and nothing of G = [a+1, 2j-1].  (Anything below a or above 2j-1 is
allowed.)  Then D is dead.
  a = 1: the blocks {1, 2j, 2j+1} of `Live.not_extends_block`;  a = 2: B4 (also proved directly,
  `not_coReach_b4`, with the mirror `not_coReach_b4_mirror`).
Proof.  Work on the background D.  At any moment let p = number of placed panels in [1, a-1]
(including those of D; p <= a-1) and s = number of placed panels of G.
INVARIANT: every placed panel of G lies in [j+1, j+p], and s <= p.  It holds at the start
(s = 0).  Placing a panel below a raises p; placing a panel above 2j - 1 changes nothing.  Let a
panel m of G be placed (`anchor_step`):
  * m <= j (left half, window [1, 2m-1]): its left arm contains a and the p placed small panels,
    its right arm (m, 2m-1] lies inside G (no block, nothing of D), so holds at most s <= p
    placed panels: p + 1 > s, not valid.
  * m = j + t, right half: its right arm [m+1, N] contains the whole block (2a panels); the left
    arm holds at most p + 1 + s <= 2p + 1 <= 2a - 1 (everything placed below 2j).  Not valid.
  * m = j + t, left half, t > p: the left arm holds at most p + 1 + s <= 2p + 1, the right arm
    contains [2j, 2j + 2p + 1] (as 2t >= 2p + 2 and 2a >= 2p + 2).  Not valid.
  * m = j + t, left half, t <= p (so m is in [j+1, j+p]): with b = placed G below m (<= t - 1, all
    in [j+1, m-1]) and c = placed G above m: left <= p + 1 + b, right >= 2t + c (the block part
    [2j, 2j+2t-1] and the placed G above m, all inside (m, 2m-1]).  Validity gives
    c <= p + 1 + b - 2t, so s + 1 <= b + c + 1 <= 2b + p + 2 - 2t <= p.
So s <= p <= a - 1 forever, while |G| = 2j - 1 - a >= a: G is never completed.  QED
The (2a+1)-sets anchorSet a j = {a} u [2j, 2j+2a-1] are dead (`not_coReach_anchorSet`,
`card_anchorSet`); with j = a + 1 and N >= 6a + 1 all elements lie in [a, N - a]
(`exists_dead_far`).  For a = 2: `not_coReach_b4set`, and `not_finite_exceptions_five`: no
finite family E contains, for all N, all dead 5-subsets of [2, N-1].
Sharpness (ek2 N set ...): the block must have 2a panels: {3} u [10, 14] (N = 20) is live; and
j >= a + 1 is needed: {2, 4, 5, 6, 7} and {3, 6, ..., 11} (j = a, N = 20) are live.  The relaxation is local: bfamily.py a j checks
the invariant on all reachable local states (a <= 4).
Interpretation ("endpoint reserve").  The small panels [1, a-1] (their windows are tiny and
nested at the end) are the only panels that can rebalance the interior panels against a block,
and each of them rebalances one step.  A block of 2a panels next to an anchor a needs a panels
of reserve but only a - 1 exist.  The end panel 1 is the first unit of this reserve; a set
avoiding 1 and N keeps one unit at each end, which is exactly enough for every block of
size <= 2 + (what the rest of D supplies) when k <= 4 -- and not for a 4-block anchored at 2.


4. The other families for k = 6 (numerical)
-------------------------------------------
Besides B_2 "plus anything" (1053 of the 1137 dead 6-sets of [2, N-1], N <= 32), the remaining 84
all contain 2 or N - 1:
  F1  {2, x, x+1, y, y+1, N-1} with both anchors 2 and N-1 (two-ended, like the pair {1, N}),
      e.g. {2,3,4,7,8,N-1} (N odd >= 17), {2,4,5,8,9,N-1} (N even >= 20), {2,5,6,9,10,N-1},
      {2,3,4,9,10,N-1}, {2,4,5,10,11,N-1}, {2,6,7,10,11,N-1}, {2,7,8,11,12,N-1}, ...
  F2  {2, 2j, 2j+1, 2j+4, 2j+5, z} (and {2, 2j, 2j+1, 2j+6, 2j+7, z}) with 2j around 0.6 N to
      0.7 N: a split 4-block in the right half, where the right arms reach N.
Both grow linearly in N at fixed shape, so for k = 6 the dead sets meeting {2, N-1} number
Theta(N^2) up to N = 32, and the only dead 6-subset of [3, N-2] is S_3.


5. The symmetric sporadic family S_a (numerical)
------------------------------------------------
S_a = {a, N+1-a} u [2a+2, N-2a-1] at N = 6a (block of 2a-2) and at N = 6a + 1 (block of 2a-1):
  a = 1: {1, 6} (N = 6), {1, 4, 7} (N = 7)  -- the known dead pair {1, N} and the N = 7 sporadic;
  a = 2: {2, 6, 7, 11} (N = 12, the exception of E_4), {2, 6, 7, 8, 12} (N = 13);
  a = 3: {3, 8, ..., 11, 16} (N = 18), {3, 8, ..., 12, 17} (N = 19);
  a = 4: {4, 10, ..., 15, 21} (N = 24), {4, 10, ..., 16, 22} (N = 25);
  a = 5: {5, 12, ..., 19, 26} (N = 30), {5, 12, ..., 20, 27} (N = 31);  a = 6: N = 36.
All dead by exhaustive search (sym.py: all reachable states on the background, e.g. 1688 for
a = 5; ek2 N set ...); the non-symmetric variants tested ({4, 10..15, 22} at N = 25 etc.) are live.
sym.py shows the mechanism: the two sides are copies of B_a with j = a + 1 whose blocks
[2a+2, 4a+1] are truncated by two panels, the two missing panels being the innermost panels of
the other side's G; at most 2a of the 2a + 2 panels of G_L u G_R are ever placed.  Each S_a is a
single set, so for fixed k it is a finite exception: k = 2a at N = 3k, and k = 2a + 1 at
N = 3k - 2 (min = a = c_k, inside the radius, so not an exception of E*).


6. Revised conjecture E*_k and the counting bound
-------------------------------------------------
CONJECTURE E*_k.  Let c_k = max(1, floor((k-1)/2)).  For every N, every k-subset of
[c_k + 1, N - c_k] is co-reachable, except for k even >= 4 the set S_{k/2} at N = 3k.
Evidence: sections 1, 5 and (k7lo4.txt, k8lo4.txt):
  k = 2: [2, N-1], N <= 100: none.     k = 3: [2, N-1], N <= 100: none.
  k = 4: [2, N-1], N <= 40: S_2 only.  k = 5: [3, N-2], N <= 40: none.
  k = 6: [3, N-2], N <= 32: S_3 only.  k = 7: [4, N-3], N <= 28: RESULT_K7
  k = 8: [4, N-3], N <= 24: RESULT_K8
Necessity of the radius: anchorSet a (a+1) (k = 2a + 1) has least element a = c_k, and adding
elements above 2j - 1 keeps it dead, so for every k >= 3 there are dead k-sets with least
element c_k (for N >= 3k or so); thus [c_k + 1, N - c_k] cannot be enlarged to [c_k, N + 1 - c_k].
COROLLARY (Lean `card_dead_le` with c = c_k, E = {S_{k/2}}):  E*_k implies
   #{dead k-subsets of [N]} <= 2 c_k C(N-1, k-1) + 1 <= (k - 1) C(N-1, k-1) + 1 = O(N^{k-1}),
and since the valid k-prefixes number ~ c'_k N^k (experiments/prefix), live/valid -> 1.
Numerically the dead count is much smaller: Theta(N^{k-2}) (livek section 5), because most
dead sets need TWO elements fixed near an end (anchor + block start), not one.


7. Proof strategies (question 2)
--------------------------------
(a) Why avoiding the ends helps: proven content is sections 2-3.  The end panels are always valid
    (empty windows), and more generally the panels near an end have small nested windows; they
    form a reserve of "balancing tokens".  A dead configuration near the left end is an anchor a
    with a block of 2a panels at [2j, 2j+2a-1]; the reserve below a has a - 1 tokens, one short.
    Avoiding 1 keeps a reserve of one panel at each end; a one-ended obstruction then needs an
    anchor >= 2 and a block of >= 4 panels, i.e. k >= 5 -- which is why E_k holds for k <= 4
    (numerically) with only the two-ended symmetric S_2 as exception, and fails for k = 5.
(b) "Endpoint reserve" as a positive statement.  The naive form is false in both directions:
    placing 1 and N FIRST (CoReach(D u {1, N})) fails already for D = {} with N even ({1, N}
    dead), and placing them LAST fails for D = {} at N = 4 (2, 3 cannot be ordered with 1, 4
    unplaced).  Placing 1 adds +1 to the left arm of every left-half panel at once, i.e. it
    shifts the imbalance I_D by +1 on the whole left half; Theorem G needs I_D <= 0 left of the
    centre, so on the background the end panels should be placed as late as possible -- but the
    outermost-panel argument of the greedy (an arm is full) needs them present.  A workable
    version would be a greedy with a budget: run the centre greedy, and whenever the outermost
    unplaced panel of the left half has an unbalanced arm, spend a reserve panel (1, then 2,
    ...); the B_a invariant (s <= p) is exactly the bookkeeping such a proof would need, and it
    shows the budget available near an end is a - 1 below an anchor a.
(c) Reduction of D u {1} to a smaller strip by a copy transfer: the near-copy and Beatty/centred
    copies of live3r/live5-7 transfer co-reachability from an n-strip to the N-strip through
    window embeddings; D u {1} for D far from the ends does not lie in any proven copy family
    in general (the anchored blocks show that "far from the ends" is not even enough for
    deadness to be excluded), so a copy route would have to be restricted to sets satisfying a
    reserve condition like E*_k's radius.  Not pursued further.
(d) Induction on N by removing the ends: for m in [2, N-1] the window in the N-strip is the window
    of m - 1 in the (N-2)-strip plus the two outer panels m - reach, m + reach (= 1 and 2m - 1 in
    the left half).  So the N-condition is the (N-2)-condition plus a correction of
    [1 placed] - [2m-1 placed] for left-half m: the panel 1 enters uniformly, but 2m - 1 does not;
    the correction is the ThinCover-style "line with bounded offset", and it is not monotone in
    the placed set.  We found no clean implication "co-reachable in the (N-2)-strip => co-reachable
    in [N]" (it fails for D = {} type cases by parity).
(e) What a proof of E*_k should look like: a classification of LOCAL obstructions near one end
    (B_a-type: an anchor and a block exceeding the reserve), of two-ended ones (F1, S_a), plus a
    constructive completion (Theorem G after spending reserve panels) for everything else.  The
    proven half is the obstruction side for one-ended blocks.
