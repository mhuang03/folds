1. Near copies (Lean `NearEmb`, `coReach_nearEmb`)
-------------------------------------------------
A map phi : [p] -> [N] (injective, into [N]) is REGULAR at a panel j of [p] if for every
y in [p], phi(y) lies in the left (right) arm of phi(j) in [N] iff y lies in the left (right)
arm of j in [p].  A window copy is regular everywhere; a near copy only outside a small set.

THEOREM 1 (near transfer).  If the complement Y = [N] \ phi([p]) has an ordering from scratch
(CoReach N phi([p])), D is co-reachable in [p] and phi is regular at every panel of [p] \ D,
then phi(D) is co-reachable in [N].
Proof: order Y first, then phi(beta), beta an ordering of [p] \ D from scratch.  Around a
regular panel phi(j), the image phi([p]) is balanced (its arms are the images of the arms of j,
which have equally many panels), the full strip is balanced, so Y is balanced; and the arms of
phi(j) restricted to the image are the images of the arms of j.  So the step phi(j) is valid
iff j is.  The panels of D are never stepped, so phi need not be regular there.  QED
(This is coReach_winEmb of Live2 with the regularity required only outside D.)

Typical near copies: a copy (Beatty, centred Beatty, residue class) with one or two panels
moved by one.  Moving the panel y0 of the odd class y -> 2y - 1 (N = 2p - 1) by d = +-1 makes
y0 itself irregular and at most one more panel: for d = -1 the panel j with 2j - p = y0
(left end of its arm), for d = +1 the panel j = (y0 + 1)/2 (right end of its arm); none when
the parities do not allow it.  If the moved panels and these exceptions lie in D, Theorem 1
applies.

THEOREM 2 (complement through a second near copy; Lean `coReach_compl_nearEmb`).  If
psi : [q] -> [N] is regular except at i1, i2, [i1, i2] is a live 2-prefix of [q] and
[psi i1, psi i2] a valid 2-prefix of [N], then psi([q]) has an ordering from scratch: the image
of a completion [i1, i2, ...] of the prefix (steps after the first two are at regular panels,
so near_step of Live4 applies).  Liveness of [i1, i2] is Conjecture C (ThinCover.conjectureC).


2. X_N for all odd N (Lean `coReach_X_odd`, `coReach_chiX_transfer`)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
Live5 showed X_N = chiX([n-1]) = {1, 4, 6, ..., N-3, N} co-reachable for N = 2n - 1, n even,
through psiX (1 -> 2, n -> N - 1, y -> 2y - 1), which needs a full sequence of [n] placing 1
before 2 and n before n - 1.  For n odd (N = 1 mod 4) take [1, n] ++ sigma.reverse, sigma an
ordering of [n] \ {1, n} from scratch ({1, n} is co-reachable for odd n != 7, Live5
coReach_one_N; [1, n] is valid since both panels have reach 0).  So chiX transfers for every
n >= 6, n != 7 (coReach_winEmb).
Consequence (`coReach_one_even_N`): {1, 2k, N} = chiX({1, k, n-1}); {1, k, n-1} is always
valid in [n-1] and is dead only for odd n-1 and k in {2, n-2}.


3. The decidable rules (Lean `nearTrB`, `nearTrP`, `chiXB`; C: rcov.c)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
nearTrB N a b c k p u v y1 e1 y2 e2 i j l c2 ms: phi = copyF k N p u v with the panels y1, y2
moved by +1 (e = 1) or -1 (e = 2) (y = 0: no move); phi increasing into [N]; (a, b, c) =
phi(i, j, l) with (i, j, l) valid and not dead in [p], p < N, p != 7 (so co-reachable by the
induction hypothesis Good3 p); phi regular outside {i, j, l} (regB, O(p^2)); and the image
satisfies Theorem G after the explicit valid steps ms (stepsB), so its complement is ordered
from scratch.  nearTrP: the same with Theorem 2 for the complement (psi = the increasing
enumeration of Y, irregular at most at i1, i2).  chiXB: the transfer of section 2.
copyOK is NOT required: regularity is checked directly, so any base map can be used.
rcov.c searches these hints (bases: centred Beatty Z(N,p), right-aligned Beatty, residue
classes; moves only on the panels i, j, l; up to 4 steps for the image; the triple or its
mirror image).  genchk.py / chkall.py turn the hints of rcall.txt into `decide +kernel`
examples against Folds.Live3r; all 4208 hints for N = 51..70 check (chkall.txt).

4. A closed-form family for all N: {1, 2, c}, N = 1 mod 4, c odd (`coReach_one_two_odd`)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
phi12: 1 -> 1, 2 -> 2, y -> 2y - 1 (the odd class of N = 2p - 1 with 3 moved to 2), p odd.
  * phi12 is regular at every panel except 2 (omega on the closed forms; for p even the
    panel (p+2)/2 would be irregular too, its arm ends at 2 in [p] but not in [N]).
  * Its image B = {1, 2, 5, 7, ..., N}: 4 is a valid step (arms {1,2,3}, {5,6,7}: 2 and 2),
    and B + {4} pulls no panel to the left: with F(t) = #(B+{4}) n [0, t) =
    t/2 + 1 (t >= 5), 2 (t = 3, 4), t - 1 (t <= 2), the arms of m count F(m) - F(m - r)
    and F(m + 1 + r) - F(m + 1), and the left count is never smaller (omega).  Theorem G
    with the centre 1/2, then Theorem 2.
  * {1, 2, c} = phi12({1, 2, (c+1)/2}); {1, 2, g} is valid in [p], and not dead for
    g <= p - 2 (the dead sets {1, 2, p-1}, {1, 2, p} of the odd strip p).
Checked numerically for every N = 1 mod 4 up to 201 (fam12odd.c).
The even c = 0 mod 4 (same N) work with a second move (c - 1 -> c, panel c/2) and the steps
4, (N+3)/2 (rcov hints), but that is not formalized in closed form.


5. What is left: the family {1, 2, c}
------------------------------------
The 86 triples (N <= 70) not settled by Covered3R, all of the form {1, 2, c} (canonical
orientation; the mirror image {N+1-c, N-1, N} is the same set up to mirror):
   N=51: c = 44 48 49        N=53: 46 50 51            N=54: 53
   N=55: 52 53               N=57: 46 50 52 54 55      N=58: 57
   N=59: 33..57 except 39 42 (23 values)                N=61: 50 54 56 58 59
   N=62: 61                  N=63: 56 58 60 61         N=64: c = 64, and {1, 63, 64}
   N=65: 62 63               N=66: 65
   N=67: 37..65 except 43 46 (27 values)                N=69: 58 62 64 66 67
   N=70: 69
Pattern: {1, 2, N-2} for every odd N; {1, 2, N-1} for N = 2 mod 4; {1, 2, N}, {1, N-1, N}
for N = 64; for N = 1 mod 4 and N = 7 mod 8 some even c close to N (mostly c = 2 mod 4);
for N = 3 mod 8 (59, 67; 51 is saved by accidents of Z(51, p)) essentially every
c >= 0.55 N.

Why the tools stop here.  Any (near) copy containing the adjacent panels 1, 2 starts with
phi(1) = 1, phi(2) = 2 and has ratio about 2.  For N = 2p - 1 with p even (N = 3 mod 4) the
odd class with 3 -> 2 is irregular at j* = (p + 2)/2 (partner 2), and j* is not in the
preimage {1, 2, g}; the other ratio-2 maps (Z(N, p) for p near N/2) have irregular panels
in pairs (e.g. N = 59, Z(59, 31): panels 11 and 21 irregular against each other).  rcov
with the complement condition dropped (NOY=1) still finds no admissible near map for 66 of
the 86 triples, so no better proof of the complement can help; one valid step before the
near copy (step4.c, preimage a 4-set made inward in [p] by <= 2 steps) finds nothing
either.  Theorem G after k explicit steps needs k >= 5 for c >= 0.75 N (steps.c, depth.c);
the DFS completions (pfx.c) show a halving chain of steps
   (N+c)/2 + 1, about half of it, ..., 3   (e.g. N = 59, c = 50: 53 27 23 14 12 8 7 5 4 3 37 36)
after which the background is inward.  A proof for all N would have to formalize such a
chain of length ~log N (an induction on the chain), or a ghost transfer: at the irregular
panel j* the condition becomes I_T(j*) = -1 instead of 0, i.e. co-reachability of
{1, 2, g} in [p] in a strip where 2 is invisible from j*; neither is carried out.

So: Residual3R N for all N > 50 is equivalent to the co-reachability of the triples of
{1, 2, c} type (and their mirrors) that Covered3R misses, which for N <= 70 are exactly the
list above.  All 86 are co-reachable numerically: leftchk.txt gives for each a list of
valid steps after which the background satisfies Theorem G (pfx.c, checked by vsteps.py),
except {1, 2, 67} at N = 69, found by a longer DFS (hard69.txt, 57 steps).  The members
{1, 2, N-2} (N odd) are the hardest: the near copy of section 4 maps them to the DEAD set
{1, 2, p-1} of [p].  `forall N > 50, Residual3 N` is NOT proven.
